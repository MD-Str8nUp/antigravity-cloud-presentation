# Vibe coding and skill-driven engineering solve different stages of software discovery

Neither Baseline Vibe-Coding nor Skill-Driven Engineering (`/wayfinder`) is universally right or wrong. Vibe coding (guided only by a global clarifying-question rule) is ideal for rapidly exploring potential approaches and seeing a working prototype in 15 minutes when requirements are still fuzzy, whereas `/wayfinder` and `mattpocock/skills` shine when you are ready to engineer a maintainable system with durable documentation (`GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/adr/`, `spec.md`) and automated tests.
