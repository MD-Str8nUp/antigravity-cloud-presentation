# 07: Live Multi-Build Shopping List, Supplier Quick-Order Exports & Order Receiving

**What to build:** The live **Shopping List** view that aggregates unfulfilled **BOM Line** demand across any user-selected combination of **Kit Builds** before subtracting **Available Stock** to compute **Net Shortfall**, an optional **"Include catalog low-stock restocking"** toggle, expandable per-build and reference-designator traceability, inline overrides for `Order Quantity`, `Supplier`, and `Supplier Part #`, **Full Shopping List CSV** and **Supplier Quick-Order CSV / Clipboard** exports, and 1-click **"Receive Selected Lines into Stock"** when orders arrive.

**Blocked by:** 03: Kit Builds, BOM Line Auto-Matching & 5-State Stock Fulfillment

**Status:** resolved

- [x] Selecting any combination of **Kit Builds** on the **Shopping List** view aggregates every non-kit **BOM Line** (`fulfillment_status NOT IN ('Included in Kit', 'Installed', 'Picked')`) with $\text{unallocated\_needed} = \max(0, \text{quantity\_required} - \text{quantity\_allocated})$, grouping across all selected builds by `component_id` when linked or by normalized `(category, canonical_value_display, package_or_mounting)` when unlinked, and computes **Net Shortfall** across the combined demand before subtracting **Available Stock**:
  $$\text{Net Shortfall} = \max\left(0, \sum \text{unallocated\_needed} - \text{Available Stock}\right)$$
- [x] Enabling the **"Include catalog low-stock restocking"** toggle adds any catalog **Component** where $(\text{Available Stock} - \sum \text{unallocated\_needed}) < \text{low\_stock\_threshold}$ and tops up its recommended order quantity by $\text{low\_stock\_threshold} - (\text{Available Stock} - \sum \text{unallocated\_needed})$.
- [x] Expanding any aggregated row on the **Shopping List** reveals the exact contributing **Kit Builds** and `reference_designators`, and the builder can inline-edit `Order Quantity` (initialized to **Net Shortfall** plus any restock top-up) and select or override `Supplier` and `Supplier Part #` (pre-filled from existing **Stock Lot** metadata on linked **Components**).
- [x] A builder can export the **Shopping List** either as a **Full Shopping List CSV** (all columns, optionally filtered by supplier) or as a supplier-filtered **Supplier Quick-Order CSV / Clipboard format** (`Supplier Part #, Order Quantity, Customer Reference`) ready to paste or upload into Mouser, DigiKey, Tayda, or LCSC cart tools.
- [x] Triggering **"Receive Selected Lines into Stock"** with a chosen target **Storage Location** creates or increments **Stock Lots** by the received quantity (merging into any existing lot at that location) and automatically creates and links catalog **Components** for any previously unlinked **BOM Lines**.
- [x] Integration tests against `:memory:` Turso verify multi-build shared-stock **Net Shortfall** calculation (ensuring **Available Stock** is never double-counted across builds), low-stock restock top-up math, inline order quantity and supplier overrides, Full and Supplier Quick-Order CSV formatting, and receiving lines into **Stock Lots** with automatic unlinked **BOM Line** linking.
